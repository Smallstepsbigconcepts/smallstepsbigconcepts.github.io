Poker & humor shirts

The story

Before → turning point → after

Before

Shelved in 2015

I started a T-shirt experiment in 2015. I had designs made for Merch by Amazon, opened a Printful store, wrote research notes… and then shelved it.

Turning point

Three test shirts

On Sep 29, 2026, three test shirts went live on eBay through Printful. Seven are on eBay now.

After

55 listings live

The AfterTheRiverPoker shop on Etsy had 55 shirt listings live as of Nov 21, 2026: poker, chess, humor and one holiday design, each its own listing. The first seven are also on eBay.

How we do it

The rules I actually follow

  • Zero trademark terms.
  • Check dark designs against a black background before uploading.
  • One approved master file per design.
Lessons learned

Failures included

  • Listing a shirt isn't marketing it. Traffic is the only real lever.
  • Since mid-November a short video with a shirt link goes out on the AfterTheRiver YouTube channel most days. 174 visits in 30 days and no orders yet shows how far traffic still has to go.
