Before

The list stays in your head

Most of us keep the year's jobs in our heads. That's why the water heater never gets drained and the insurance renews at whatever price they send.

Turning point

A cascade, like the one I used in the Navy

I spent 20 years on submarines. Maintenance didn't run on anyone's memory. It ran on a schedule that cascades down: yearly, then every quarter, then weekly, then daily. You only have to look at today's list.
