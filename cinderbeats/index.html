The story

Before → turning point → after

Before

Stuck on Twitch

Hundreds of highlights lived only on Twitch: 328 highlights and 141 clips. Clipping and posting had been tried four different ways, and none of them stuck.

Turning point

One clean system

As her business partner on the behind-the-scenes side, I set up one dedupe log keyed to each highlight, so nothing gets posted twice, plus a curation checklist: 5 criteria scored 0–2, keep anything that scores 7 or more.

After

Clips going out

New clips go out every day on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube Shorts, with the next three weeks already scheduled.

How we do it

The rules I actually follow

  • Only clips she made herself go out as-is, and anything new needs her OK first.
  • No editing her clips.
  • Nothing with copyrighted sound alerts, private moments, or name-calling.
  • Posts go out in her voice, not mine.
Lessons learned

Failures included

  • Her 130 YouTube Shorts have a median of 55 views, versus 5 for her 249 regular videos. Format matters more than volume.
  • One clip got uploaded twice because nobody checked what she had already posted. Now we check first.
