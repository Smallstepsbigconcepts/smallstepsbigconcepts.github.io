Project · stalled

30-Day Identity Challenge

The one that stalled. A daily Facebook challenge from late 2025 about small wins: daily small actions, then a shift in identity, then self-trust.

The story

Before → turning point → after

Before

Prove it

I wanted to prove that small daily steps change who you are.

Turning point

What happened

Day 1 “Small Win,” Day 2 “Bubble Sheet,” Day 3 “Clarification,” Day 4 “Do It Messy.” I posted 16 days by Dec 1, 2025 and finished writing Day 17, “It's Too Small (The Lie That Keeps You Stuck).” Then it stopped, even with a daily 5 PM reminder set.

After

Where it stands

Days 17–21 have a framework and Days 22–30 are outlined. I didn't keep my own copies of Days 1–16, so they're only on Facebook.

Lessons learned

Why a reminder wasn't enough

  • A reminder isn't a system.
  • A daily public promise needs a finish plan and a buffer for bad days.
  • Keep your own copy of everything you post.
